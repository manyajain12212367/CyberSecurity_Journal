# 📓 Cybersecurity Journal

Welcome to my **Cybersecurity Journal** — a living record of my learning, exploration, and growth in the field of cybersecurity.

This repository exists because I learned that **journaling makes learning stick**. Instead of passively consuming content, I’m documenting my journey to build clarity, depth, and strong fundamentals.

---

## 🧭 Why This Journal?

During my **B.Tech CSE**, I explored multiple domains to understand what truly interested me. Cybersecurity eventually *clicked*, and by **September 2025**, I realized my strongest inclination lies in **defensive security — particularly malware and memory analysis**.

Now, I’m intentionally **restarting from the basics** to rebuild my knowledge cleanly and structurally — replacing scattered understanding with a solid foundation.

This journal reflects that reset.

---

## 🚀 What This Journal Covers

* 📘 **Fundamentals** — starting with **CompTIA Security+ Objectives**
* 🧠 **Defensive Security** — malware analysis, memory forensics, DFIR concepts
* 🛠️ **Tools & Techniques** — hands-on notes from tools I explore and revisit
* 🔍 **Case-style Write-ups** — how I think, analyze, fail, learn, and improve
* 🧪 **Experiments & Observations** — breaking things to understand them better

---

## 📌 Philosophy

* Learn deeply, not quickly
* Build intuition, not just notes
* Understand *why* something works, not just *how*
* Document progress — including confusion, mistakes, and breakthroughs
* Lets Not think "I can not do it", but rather "How can i do it?"
